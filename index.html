About Me

Engineer by training,
builder by passion.

Two disciplines, one mindset — understand how things work, then make them work better.

I'm Golam Mahamud — a Mechanical Engineer and Web Developer / Designer from Bangladesh.

My foundation is in mechanical engineering — practical, hands-on knowledge of tools, software, technologies, and industry resources, sharpened through a Diploma in Engineering. That training taught me precision, systems thinking, and how to solve real problems under real constraints.

Alongside it, I build for the web: modern, responsive, user-friendly websites with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React.js — with a designer's eye for clean UI/UX. I actively explore AI-powered tools and platforms to work smarter and ship better.

Whether it's a machine or a website, my approach is the same: design it well, build it right, make it useful.
